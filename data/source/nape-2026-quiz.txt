80 quick questions, thoroughly mixed so geology, drilling and production, health and safety, and the oil and gas business are all jumbled together. They are split into four sets of 20 for easy hosting. Most questions have four options; a few are true or false. The correct option has a green tick, and the answer key with a fun fact for each question is at the end.

Tip for hosts: read the fun fact aloud after each answer. That's where the learning happens.

Fun fact

Nigeria's deepwater fields use FPSOs, such as those at Bonga and Egina.

8

Casing protects groundwater zones and holds the hole open.

9

10

11

12

14

20

A) Tiny marine plants and animals (plankton)

Sorry, dinosaur fans. Most oil comes from microscopic sea life buried in mud.
